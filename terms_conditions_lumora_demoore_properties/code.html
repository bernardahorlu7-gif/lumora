Master agreement / revised protective draft

Client & Project Services Agreement

This agreement applies to a project only when accepted in writing together with its Project Schedule, quotation, BOQ, scope of work, or work order.

Important draft notice

This is a negotiating draft prepared to protect Lumora DeMoore Properties' interests. It is not a substitute for review by a Ghana-qualified construction lawyer. The liability cap, retention, tax, arbitration, subcontract flow-down, and payment provisions should be checked before use or signature.

1. Parties and role

This Agreement is between LUMORA DEMOORE PROPERTIES, Registration No. BN674470526, TIN P006736022X, a sole proprietorship operating under the laws of Ghana with its principal place of business in Accra, and the counterparty named in the relevant Project Schedule. Lumora acts either as General Contractor, where the counterparty is the Client/Employer, or as Subcontractor, where the counterparty is the Main Contractor.

2. Purpose and document priority

Each project is governed by its accepted Project Schedule, quotation, BOQ, scope, or work order together with this Master Agreement. If documents conflict, priority is: signed variation; Project Schedule or Work Order; accepted quotation or BOQ; this Agreement; and, for subcontract work only, expressly incorporated Main Contract provisions.

3. Services

Services may include building construction, civil engineering, architectural finishing, project management, procurement, real estate development, lift services, and related work agreed for the project.

4. Written instructions and variations

Changes, additions, and out-of-scope instructions bind Lumora only when confirmed in writing, including email or WhatsApp, by an authorized counterparty representative. Urgent protective work reasonably needed to prevent loss or damage may be carried out with notice within 48 hours and is payable as a variation.

5. Price and currency

The price is stated in Ghana Cedis unless otherwise agreed and may be lump sum, remeasured, unit-rate, cost-plus, or milestone-based. Only expressly included work, quantities, and standards are covered.

6. Payment

Before mobilization, the deposit is 70% where Lumora supplies materials and 50% where it does not. The balance follows the agreed milestones and retention terms. Invoices are due within 7 days unless the Project Schedule states otherwise. Overdue sums accrue interest at 3% per month or the prevailing Bank of Ghana policy rate plus 5% per annum, whichever is higher, calculated daily. Lumora is not required to mobilize or order materials before cleared payment.

7. Default and suspension

After written notice of non-payment, Lumora may suspend personnel, procurement, and delivery. Resumption requires all overdue sums, accrued interest, and reasonable remobilization costs of not less than 5% of suspended works. Lumora is not liable for consequences of the counterparty's late payment.

8. Counterparty-caused delay

Delay caused by late payment, late decisions or approvals, restricted access, interference, instruction, default, or other counterparty acts or omissions entitles Lumora to an extension of time, direct delay costs including idle labour, equipment, supervision, storage, security and remobilization, and price adjustment for cost increases during the delay. Lumora should notify the counterparty within 7 days of awareness with supporting records where requested; late notice does not automatically forfeit a claim but may affect recovery where prejudice is proven.

9-12. Counterparty duties, site conditions, and materials

The counterparty must provide access, accurate information, assigned permits and approvals, timely decisions, utilities, facilities, and coordination. Lumora is not responsible for hidden structural defects, unrecorded services, contaminated substrates, unforeseen ground conditions, or defective prior work that could not reasonably have been identified. Counterparty-supplied materials remain the counterparty's responsibility for suitability, quantity, genuineness, fitness, and timely availability; unsuitable materials may be refused.

13-14. Escalation and programme

Where exchange rates move by more than 5%, major material costs rise by more than 7%, statutory charges increase, or a counterparty delay causes cost exposure, Lumora may adjust the price by written notice supported by evidence. Completion dates extend for counterparty delay, variations, force majeure, adverse weather, government restrictions, and unforeseen site conditions.

15-17. Workmanship, inspection, and warranty

Lumora performs agreed work with reasonable skill and care in accordance with agreed specifications, drawings, and manufacturer instructions. Defects must be notified in writing within 7 days of awareness, and Lumora receives the first reasonable opportunity to investigate and rectify. Unless the Project Schedule states otherwise, the defects period is 12 months from practical completion. The warranty excludes wear, misuse, neglect, accidental damage, structural movement not caused by Lumora, third-party work, unauthorized alterations, defective counterparty materials, and failure to follow maintenance instructions.

18-20. Retention, coordination, and safety

Default retention is 5% of the contract sum: half releases at practical completion and half at the end of the defects period, subject to reasonable deductions for notified unresolved defects. The counterparty or Main Contractor coordinates other contractors unless project management is expressly assigned to Lumora. Lumora may suspend unsafe work without breach, and damage caused after completion by others is a chargeable variation.

21-23. Documents, confidentiality, and photographs

Lumora retains ownership of its quotations, methodologies, pricing, drawings, and prepared documents until fully paid. Each party protects confidential commercial, technical, and financial information. Unless the counterparty objects in writing before commencement, Lumora may photograph completed works for records, quality assurance, marketing, and portfolio purposes, subject to agreed confidentiality restrictions.

24-25. Subcontractors and flow-down

As General Contractor, Lumora may engage necessary subcontractors and specialists, subject to any approval stated in the Project Schedule. As Subcontractor, Main Contract terms bind Lumora only when expressly identified, provided, and accepted in writing. Payment to Lumora is not conditional on the Main Contractor being paid by the Employer. An unpaid certified amount after 21 days may trigger suspension rights.

26-28. Insurance, liability, and indemnity

Each party maintains insurance assigned in the Project Schedule or required by law. The party responsible for the works should maintain contractor's all-risk or equivalent and public liability cover. Subject to liabilities that cannot lawfully be limited under Ghanaian law, Lumora's total liability is capped at the relevant project contract sum. Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss. Each party is responsible for losses arising from its own negligent, unlawful, or unauthorized acts.

29-30. Suspension and termination

Lumora may suspend for overdue payment, unsafe conditions, unavailable access or approvals, material interference, or material breach. Either party may terminate a material breach not remedied within 14 days of written notice. On termination, the counterparty pays for performed work, committed materials, variations, demobilization, applicable retention, and reasonable cancellation charges.

31-32. Disputes and force majeure

Parties first negotiate for 14 days, then mediate. If unresolved within 30 days of mediation commencing, either party may refer the dispute to arbitration in Accra under Ghana's Alternative Dispute Resolution Act, 2010 (Act 798), in English before one arbitrator. Urgent court relief remains available. Neither party is liable for qualifying events beyond reasonable control; events continuing more than 60 days may support termination with payment for work completed and committed costs.

33-35. General and governing law

Assignment requires written consent, except Lumora may assign payment rights as security to its bank or financier by notice. Notices may be by letter, email, WhatsApp, signed site instruction, or another agreed written method. The written Agreement and project documents form the entire agreement; amendments must be written. Invalid provisions are severed without invalidating the remainder. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Republic of Ghana.

36. Project Schedule

Every project schedule should state the project name and location; Lumora's role; counterparty; scope; contract type; contract sum; whether Lumora supplies materials; commencement and completion dates; progress milestones; retention; warranty period; and special conditions. Default terms are 70% deposit with materials, 50% without materials, 5% retention, and 12 months' defects liability.

37. Signatures

The authorized representative of Lumora and the counterparty should sign and date the Project Schedule and identify any witness. No project should rely on an unsigned or incomplete schedule.
