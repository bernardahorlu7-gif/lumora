Prestige Building Waterproofing

Comprehensive subterranean and exterior facade waterproofing remediation for a Class-A commercial high-rise in the downtown business district.

Overview Gallery Progress Documents

Scope of Work

The project entails the complete excavation and replacement of the existing compromised waterproofing membrane along the north and east elevations of the foundation wall. Additionally, minor concrete spalling repairs will be conducted on the exposed structural columns prior to the application of the new dual-layer bituminous membrane system.

  • Excavation to footing depth (approx. 14ft) along specified elevations.
  • Removal and disposal of existing failed membrane and rigid insulation.
  • Surface preparation including crack injection and parging.
  • Application of primary fluid-applied waterproofing membrane.
  • Installation of secondary dimpled drainage board and new weeping tile system.
  • Backfilling with clear stone and compacted native soil.
